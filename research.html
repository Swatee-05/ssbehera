CLEANROOM FABRICATION & PHOTONICS

Integrated 4H-SiC Quantum Photonic Devices

Focusing on the design, cleanroom fabrication, and optical coupling of sub-micron scale 4H-Silicon Carbide waveguides and suspended cantilever structures. Utilizing electron beam lithography (EBL) and dry plasma etching (ICP-RIE) to integrate color center emitters with optical fibers.

Implementation of warm atomic vapor magnetometry platforms using nonlinear magneto-optical rotation (NMOR). Developing digital lock-in amplifier signal processing algorithms in Python and custom hardware interfaces for sub-picotesla magnetic field sensing.

Applications: What Can You Do After Quantum Keys Are Generated?

Once Alice and Bob share a synchronized stream of quantum-generated key bits, they gain access to security capabilities impossible with classical mathematics alone:

  • Information-Theoretic Security (One-Time Pad): By combining the generated key with plaintext using XOR encryption equal to message length, data transmissions achieve mathematically unhackable secrecy guaranteed by physics laws.
  • Eavesdropping Detection: Any interception attempt by an adversary (Eve) alters the photon's quantum state, increasing the Quantum Bit Error Rate (QBER). Alice and Bob instantly detect eavesdroppers and discard compromised key streams.
  • Post-Quantum Infrastructure Protection: These keys protect critical infrastructure—financial networks, power grids, and defense communications—against future quantum computer decryption attacks (e.g., Shor's algorithm).

Photon Generation Mechanism

Photons are generated pseudo-randomly using JavaScript's browser RNG engine (Xorshift128+). Each photon is randomly assigned a path (Upper vs. Lower branch) and a polarization state (+ or x). Physical quantum networks utilize Quantum Random Number Generators (QRNGs) driven by physical quantum phase noise.
