Applications: What Can You Do After Quantum Keys Are Generated?

Once Alice and Bob share a synchronized stream of quantum-generated key bits, they gain access to security capabilities impossible with classical mathematics alone:

  • Information-Theoretic Security (One-Time Pad): By combining the generated key with plaintext using XOR encryption equal to message length, data transmissions achieve mathematically unhackable secrecy guaranteed by physics laws.
  • Eavesdropping Detection: Any interception attempt by an adversary (Eve) alters the photon's quantum state, increasing the Quantum Bit Error Rate (QBER). Alice and Bob instantly detect eavesdroppers and discard compromised key streams.
  • Post-Quantum Infrastructure Protection: These keys protect critical infrastructure—financial networks, power grids, and defense communications—against future quantum computer decryption attacks (e.g., Shor's algorithm).

Photon Generation Mechanism

Photons are generated pseudo-randomly using JavaScript's browser RNG engine (Xorshift128+). Each photon is randomly assigned a path (Upper vs. Lower branch) and a polarization state (+ or x). Physical quantum networks utilize Quantum Random Number Generators (QRNGs) driven by physical quantum phase noise.

How to Play & Comprehensive Physics Principles

This interactive simulator models an open-access Fabry-Pérot Cavity Quantum Electrodynamics (CQED) System coupled to a single quantum emitter, such as a Silicon-Vacancy (SiV) or Nitrogen-Vacancy (NV) color center embedded in 4H-SiC.

Core Mechanics & Alignment Steps

  • Spatial Alignment (Transverse Mode Matching): Click and drag the right concave mirror (M2) along the transverse axis. Transverse misalignment causes optical clipping and mode field diameter mismatch, scattering photons into non-resonant Hermite-Gaussian modes and lowering cavity quality factor.
  • Phase Tuning (Sub-Nanometer Longitudinal Control): Adjust the piezo stage slider to fine-tune the cavity length L to satisfy the longitudinal resonance condition L = n · (λ / 2), where λ is the zero-phonon line wavelength.
  • Achieving Strong Coupling: As alignment accuracy approaches sub-micron thresholds, cavity finesse (ℱ) spikes, boosting the single-photon Rabi frequency beyond intrinsic dissipation rates.

Physics Breakdown: Jaynes-Cummings Dynamics & Vacuum Rabi Splitting

The physics of single-emitter Cavity QED is governed by the Jaynes-Cummings Hamiltonian, which models the fundamental interaction between a two-level quantum emitter and a single quantized field mode:

H = ℏ ω_c a† a + (1/2) ℏ ω_a σ_z + ℏ g (a† σ_- + a σ_+)

Here, a† and a represent the photon creation and annihilation operators, σ_+ and σ_- represent the atomic raising and lowering operators, and g is the single-photon coherent coupling strength defined by:

g = ( μ / ℏ ) · √( ℏ ω_c / (2 ε_0 V_mode) )

Key Regime Thresholds & Physics Definitions

  • Mode Volume Minimization (V_mode): To maximize the electric field strength per photon, the cavity mode volume V_mode is minimized using tightly curved concave micro-mirrors.
  • The Purcell Effect (Weak Coupling): When the emitter is resonant with the cavity mode but photon leakage rate (κ) or emitter dephasing (γ) exceeds coupling strength (g < κ, γ), the system sits in the weak coupling regime. Spontaneous emission is accelerated by the Purcell Factor: F_P = (3 / 4 π²) · (λ / n)³ · (Q / V_mode).
  • Vacuum Rabi Splitting (Strong Coupling): When mirror alignment yields maximum finesse such that g > (κ, γ), energy is coherently swapped between the atom and the cavity field faster than decoherence occurs. The uncoupled transmission peak splits into two polariton polariton modes separated by twice the vacuum Rabi frequency (2g).

Solid-State Quantum Hardware Applications

High-finesse microcavity integration is essential for scaling up solid-state quantum optics architectures:

  • High-Fidelity Single-Photon Sources: Purcell suppression channels >95% of emitted luminescence into the zero-phonon line (ZPL), yielding indistinguishable single photons required for linear optical quantum computing.
  • Spin-Photon Entanglement Interface: Serves as a memory node in a quantum repeater network by entangling the spin state of an electron in Silicon Carbide (SiC) with the polarization state of an emitted flying qubit.
