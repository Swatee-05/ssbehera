Experimental Quantum Hardware & Photonic Systems Researcher

I specialize in translating fundamental quantum optics into stable, scalable hardware platforms. With 4+ years of cross-institutional research across Europe and India (LIST, Universität des Saarlandes, IISc, IISER), my work focuses on solid-state spin-photon interfaces (4H-SiC color centers), warm-vapor atomic magnetometers (OPMs), and non-classical light sources. I design and commission automated optical testbeds from scratch—integrating multi-axis sub-micron alignment, cryogenic fiber-to-chip packaging, and modular Python/MATLAB instrument automation for high-stability experimental environments.

Technical Capabilities & Hardware Infrastructure

Hands-on commissioning, cleanroom processing, and precision instrument interfacing.

Integrated Quantum Photonics & Packaging

Engineered automated waveguide-to-fiber coupling stations, qualified UV-curable optical adhesives (Dymax systems), and conducted cleanroom microfabrication and FE-SEM characterization of specialty photonic fibers (1060XP, ZBLAN).

Hardware: 6-axis piezo stages, Dymax UV curing, Hitachi SU-70 FE-SEM, Fujikura 90S fusion splicer.

Atomic Sensors & Non-Classical Optics

Constructed 85Rb optically pumped magnetometers using NMOR in Twinleaf mu-metal shielding, fabricated calibrated 3-axis Helmholtz coils, and generated polarization/path-hyperentangled photon pairs via type-I/II BBO crystals.

Hardware: Moku:Go DSP, SRS SR830 lock-in, AOM drivers (Brimrose), EMCCD (Andor iXon).

Instrumentation Control & Signal Acquisition

Developed full laboratory automation pipelines integrating synchronized optical, RF, and positioning instruments; developed custom Python digital lock-in amplifiers and low-noise data acquisition platforms.

Hardware: Swabian Time Tagger, HydraHarp/PicoHarp TCSPC, PicoScope MSO, SPAD/APDs.
