Experimental Quantum Hardware & Photonic Systems Researcher

I specialize in bridging fundamental quantum optics into robust, scalable hardware platforms. With 4+ years of cross-institutional research across Europe and India, my work spans solid-state color centers (4H-SiC), warm-vapor atomic magnetometers, and entangled photon sources. I design and build automated optical testbeds from scratch—integrating multi-axis nanometer alignment, custom Python/MATLAB instrument pipelines, and cleanroom device packaging for high-stability experimental environments.

Technical Capabilities

Integrated Quantum Photonics

Designed custom waveguide–fiber coupling stations, integrated 780–1220 nm laser platforms with 4H-SiC nanophotonic devices, and evaluated chip-level bonding protocols.

Atomic Systems & Sensing

Constructed optically pumped magnetometers using Rb atomic vapor, developed Python digital lock-in amplifiers, and characterized precision Helmholtz coil systems.

Lab Automation & Infrastructure

Built multi-instrument automated measurement pipelines using Python and MATLAB, coordinating multi-axis positioning, TCSPC systems, RF drivers, and digital oscilloscopes.

Interactive Waveguide Coupling Simulation

Interactive wave-propagation model illustrating a single-photon Gaussian pulse originating from a divacancy color center in 4H-SiC, guided via a cantilever waveguide, and evanescently coupled to a tapered SiO₂ fiber.
